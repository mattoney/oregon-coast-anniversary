Haystack tide pools. Best low of the trip.

Reading the numbers. Low tide means the water has pulled back and the rocks are exposed. High tide means it’s all the way in. The feet are measured off the average low mark, so 0.2 ft is very low and 7.3 ft is a lot of water. Tide pools want low. Thor’s Well wants high. That’s the whole thing.

The long one. Gold Beach to Pacific City, roughly 5:30 with stops.

  • 8:45 AMOut of Gold Beach
  • 12:15 PMHeceta Head from the 101 pulloutNo fee, no walk up, just the view
  • 1:15 PMCape Perpetua for Thor’s WellHigh is 2:53, and the hour before high is when it actually drains and spouts. Stay well back from the shelf, it gets swept
  • 2:15 PMInto Yachats for a short walk and coffee

  • 5:00 PMCheck in at Two Capes Lookout, Pacific City

If you want Thor’s Well at the peak. Stay until 3:00 and you’ll reach the dome closer to 5:45 instead of 5:00. Decide on the day, but the rising hour is the better show anyway.

Before you go

Seven things to handle now

  • Offline maps for the full 101 stretch. Service drops hard between Gold Beach and Florence
  • Headlamps for Thursday. You’re climbing out of Secret Beach after dark
  • Hiking shoes for God’s Thumb
  • Layers and a rain shell. The coast runs cold in July even when Medford is 95
  • Save the Pacific City food numbers now so you can order Friday from the road
  • Re-run AutoSlash before pickup in case the rate dropped since you booked
